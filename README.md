## KYC: the rule that matters when your account is stuck

Both exchanges require identity verification before normal use. The difference is what you can do before verification clears.

OKX: without verification you cannot deposit or withdraw crypto, and you cannot buy or sell on C2C. After verification, your 24-hour withdrawal limit depends on your fee tier.

Bybit: unverified accounts cannot deposit or trade, but can withdraw up to 20,000 USDT per day and 100,000 USDT per month. Standard verification raises the daily limit to 1,000,000 USDT.

If you might need to move funds while a verification review is pending, Bybit’s rule is more forgiving. If you keep most of your balance on OKX, complete verification before you need the exit.

## February 2025: two events, two different risks

On February 21, 2025, attackers took about 401,346 ETH, worth roughly $1.5 billion, from a Bybit cold wallet. The FBI attributed the theft to North Korean actors. Bybit kept withdrawals open and says it restored 1:1 backing within 72 hours. Its monthly Hacken-verified proof-of-reserves reports have continued.

On February 24, 2025, OKX’s offshore entity Aux Cayes FinTech pleaded guilty in the Southern District of New York to operating an unlicensed money transmitting business. It agreed to more than $504 million in penalties and must retain an external compliance consultant until February 2027. OKX now serves US customers through the separate FinCEN-registered OKX US.

OKX also paused withdrawals for about five weeks in October 2020 while a key holder assisted police. It says no customer funds were lost.

### What this means for a user today

Bybit’s 2025 event was a security and liquidity shock. The exchange stayed open for withdrawals and restored backing. That record matters if your main worry is a run on the exchange.

OKX’s 2025 event was a compliance failure. It paid, accepted an external compliance consultant, and kept building licensed entities. That matters if regulatory standing is your reason for choosing a venue.

Both publish monthly proof-of-reserves. OKX’s August 2026 report listed $22.96 billion in primary assets and uses zk-STARK verification. Bybit’s May 2026 Hacken-verified report showed more than $16.5 billion in mainstream assets.

Neither exchange is a cold wallet. Keep long-term holdings in self-custody.
